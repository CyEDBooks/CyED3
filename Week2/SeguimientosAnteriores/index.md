# Evaluation preparation

This section turns themes from previous activities into reusable study
material. It is organized by skill rather than by semester or grade value.

## Skills covered

- translating language requirements into formal regular expressions;
- validating complete strings with Python;
- using groups and backreferences;
- extracting repeated or structured information;
- designing positive, negative, and boundary tests; and
- explaining why a pattern satisfies each requirement.

## Recommended workflow

1. Review the [Session 2 study page](../../Week1/S2/index.md).
2. Complete the simpler patterns in the
   [Session 2 Python notebook](../../Week1/S2/regex-python.ipynb).
3. Work through the
   [evaluation-preparation notebook](regex-evaluation-prep.ipynb).
4. For each problem, write a short explanation before looking at the partial
   construction.

## What partial solutions provide

The notebook may reveal a decomposition, an appropriate Python operation, a
test strategy, or part of a pattern. It does not provide a complete answer to
every activity. The goal is to help you restart when stuck while preserving the
reasoning needed for evaluation.

:::{important}
Do not memorize finished regex strings. Be prepared to explain what each group,
class, quantifier, boundary, and backreference contributes.
:::
